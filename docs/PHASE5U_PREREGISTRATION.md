Owner's product goal (context): the owner uploads an estimation PDF; the system marks every counted member on each page like an estimator does in Bluebeam (a symbol with a tag), coloured green, amber or red by confidence; for each page and the whole job it outputs the CSV columns (Reference Dwg No., Quantity, SHAPE, Size, Name, Length ft, Length in, Unit W, Total W, Grade); the estimator reviews each page, sees what the system did AND WHY, corrects it and exports the corrected Excel. No black box: every marking must be explainable with evidence the estimator can click.

## V1 What the estimators' markings mean
- **V1a (counts, no images).** For all takeoff PDFs: counts of markups by colour (the annotation stroke colour, and the interior colour when present; each colour = the RGB triple rounded to 2 decimals) crossed with subtype, and crossed with the mark-like tag (the tag text is NOT reported; only whether a tag exists and its shape class: letters only, letters+digits, digits only, other). Per document and pooled.
- **V1b (images).** Two jobs: the job the estimator CSV belongs to (identified after the commit by matching the CSV's drawing references to the drawing sets; if more than one job fits, stop and ask) and job-018. For each job, up to 6 pages, chosen by structure only: the pages with the most count symbols (count symbol = Polygon annotation with 3 or 8 vertices, as defined in Phase 5t), ties broken by the lower page number. For each page I record, as counts and descriptions only (no drawing text): what is being counted (columns, beams, braces, joists, footings, other), what the symbol and the tag look like, and whether colour means a member type or a status (decided by whether the same colour covers several member kinds, or one kind carries several colours, over the 12 pages). Images stay in `fixtures/private/`.

## V2 How a mark appears on the drawing
From the same 12 pages and the Phase 5t M2 results: (a) is the tag printed once per piece or once per type (TYP)? answered by counting, on each page, the printed tags against the count symbols of that estimator tag; (b) how the drawing shows a repeated member (TYP note, repeated tags, a schedule quantity, a grid or bay note, other). Reported as counts of pages per answer, plus the M2 bands restated for context.

## V3 Where each CSV value comes from
- **Population.** The MAIN-steel rows of the estimator CSV: the rows the CSV itself groups or labels as main steel (its own section or category column). If it has no such label, the rows whose SHAPE is a main member shape, with the rule recorded in the results. Rows are taken in file order.
- **Sample.** 15 rows, drawn with `random.Random(5021).sample(range(n), 15)` where n is the number of MAIN-steel rows and the indexes are 0-based positions in file order. Seed 5021, drawn once, not redrawn. If n < 15, all rows.
- **Per row.** Trace to the drawing and record the source type of (i) Size: schedule, legend, plan label, none found; (ii) Length: schedule column, a dimension on the plan, a measurement (a markup in the takeoff), none found. Report counts per source type only; no sheet numbers, no text. Rows that cannot be traced are counted as "none found", with the share, not dropped.

Also a text proposal to replace the blocking gates (class confirmation, sheet decisions) by flags: the pipeline runs automatically after upload with the best guesses, everything unconfirmed is marked, and the totals read "draft, N sheets unconfirmed". ## W1 The target workbook (aggregates only, no strings from it)
- **Sheet identification.** The main-steel sheet(s) are the sheet(s) whose header row contains the words Reference Dwg No., Quantity, SHAPE, Size, Name, Length, Unit W, Total W, Grade. Cost, price and rate sheets are identified by sheet NAME and header words only and their cells are never read.
- **Reported (counts and shares only):** rows; which of the header words are present; how rows are grouped (one row per size, per name, per mark, other: decided by whether Size, Name or Name+Size is unique across rows, stated as the shares of rows with a unique value); share of rows per SHAPE value (SHAPE values are generic steel family codes and are reported as codes); number of distinct Reference Dwg No. values; Name pattern classes with counts (letters+digits, profile-like by the worker's profile parser, digits only, other, empty); Quantity bands (1; 2 to 4; 5 to 9; 10 to 24; 25 or more); total length bands in feet = Length ft + Length in / 12 (below 5; 5 to under 10; 10 to under 20; 20 to under 40; 40 or more); share of rows with a Unit W; number of distinct Grade codes with counts; share of rows where Total W equals Quantity x (Length ft + Length in / 12) x Unit W within 1% (rows with any of the four missing are counted as "not checkable").
- **Sections.** Whether section or category label rows exist (rows with a text in the first cells and no Quantity); the counts per label class (beams, columns, braces, joists, misc, other; by keyword) and whether joists are present as rows.
- **Folder.** The other files of the same folder by type and size only; whether drawing PDFs are among them.
- **Tie to a job.** Reference values are normalised (upper case, only letters and digits kept). (A) Compared with the sheet numbers listed for job-001 in docs/SPEC.md (not printed). (B) For each of our 19 unique drawing documents and each of the 21 takeoff PDFs: a reference is *found* in a document when its normalised form equals the normalised text of a whole text-layer line on some page. Reported per document: the number of distinct references found, out of the distinct references of the workbook. A document is a *tie* when at least half of the distinct references are found (A) or (B); otherwise "no match". If job-001 matches, the report says that its text is outlined (glyph reader only) and what that means for an end-to-end test.

## W2 V3 as registered (seed 5021)
Population, sample and seed as in V3 above: MAIN-steel rows of the workbook in file order, `random.Random(5021).sample(range(n), 15)`, drawn once. If W1 identifies a drawing set, each sampled row is traced on the drawing by viewing images (approved). The source of Size is one of: schedule, legend, plan label, none found; the source of Length is one of: schedule column, a dimension on the plan, a measurement, none found. Counts per source type only. If no drawing set is identified, W2 is reported as not possible with the reason.

## W3 M7: does Quantity equal the number of size labels on a page?
- **Pages.** All pages of the takeoff PDFs matched to the 16 jobs (Phase 5t inventory) that have at least one count symbol and a text layer (at least 20 non-space characters; annotations removed in memory before reading the text).
- **Quantities per page.** n_sym = Polygon annotations with 3 or 8 vertices; n_circle = those with 8 vertices; n_lbl = profile occurrences found by the worker's profile parser (canonical) in text-layer LINE items outside the title-block strip (items whose centre lies in the right 13% of the displayed page width are excluded; a line with two profiles counts two); n_lbl_W = the subset of the W family.
- **Measures.** r = |n_lbl - n_circle| / n_circle and r_W = |n_lbl_W - n_circle| / n_circle, per page with n_circle >= 1. Bands: equal (0), up to 10%, up to 25%, up to 50%, beyond. Pages with no circle symbols are counted apart ("no circles") and are not in the bands. Reported pooled, per job, and for the six viewed job-018 pages separately.
- **Reading of the circles.** Circles are taken as beams ONLY on the six job-018 pages viewed in Phase 5u; elsewhere they are only "circle symbols" and a band says nothing about beams.
- **Not done:** no tuning, no new rule, no change to the frozen rule or thresholds. The numbers are context for a counting design, nothing is chosen from them.
