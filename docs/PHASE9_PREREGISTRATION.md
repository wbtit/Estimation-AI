# Phase 9 pre-registration: Parts B and C (read-only studies)

Written and committed BEFORE any size, count or quantity was computed from the markups, and before the Part C diagnostic was run. Part D is built features with tests, not an evaluation. Deterministic code only: no LLM, no trained model. Nothing is pushed. Process rules: PIDs of anything I start are recorded and only those are stopped, no `pkill` or `pgrep -f`, the API on port 3000 is not touched, no filesystem search, no client, project or file names in code, docs, logs or chat, private data only under `fixtures/private`. When estimator files or drawings are inspected, only counts, field names and patterns are printed, never real text. No cost, price or rate cell is read. Per-markup rows stay in the git-ignored private folder.

## 0. What is already known (from Phase 5t and Phase 5v, counts only) and what is new
- 16 of our drawing documents have a marked-up takeoff PDF (20,835 markups on those 16 files; 24,229 in all 21 files).
- Phase 5t looked for a profile in Subject and Contents only, with its own parser: 0% of Polygons, FreeText tags, Ink and Circles, 3% of Squares, 36% of Highlights. It did not look at the rich-text entry (`RC`), the 15 Bluebeam column slots (`BSIColumnData`), the reply-to link between a tag and its symbol (`IRT`), the sequence number, or the layer entry (`OC`). Those are new here. A first look at key NAMES only (no values) shows `RC` and `Contents` on FreeText tags, `BSIColumnData` (15 slots) on 65% of FreeText and part of the Polygons, and `IRT` on 91% of FreeText tags.

## 1. Part B1: field inventory (descriptive, no threshold)
**Population:** every markup of the 16 matched PDFs on every page (the "all" population), and the same on the pages that map to a page of our drawing document (the "mapped" population; page map from `inventory.json`). **Count symbol** = a Polygon with 3 or 8 vertices (the Phase 5t definition). **Tag** = a FreeText markup.
**Fields read, per subtype (Polygon-symbol, Polygon-other, FreeText, Square, Highlight, Line, Ink, Circle):**
- `Subj` (subject); `Contents` (comment); `RC` (rich text, tags stripped); `T` (author, only to say whether it is filled); `BSIColumnData` slot 0 to 14 (one field per slot); the layer entry `OC` (only present or absent, with the number of distinct layer objects); colour: stroke `C` and fill `IC` as RGB, only the number of distinct colours per file and whether the colour is constant within a file.
- Standard PDF key names are printed. Slot names of Bluebeam columns are NOT in the PDF, so slots are reported as "slot k".
**Per field and subtype, reported:** markups, share filled (non-empty), number of distinct values (the values themselves are never printed), and the shares of the filled values that match each pattern class below. A value is a **whole-value match** when, after trimming, it IS a size; a **contains match** when it holds a size as a token inside longer text.
**Steel size pattern classes** (case-insensitive; `x` may be `x`, `X` or the multiplication sign; spaces ignored):
- wide-flange and similar: `W`, `M`, `S`, `HP`, `WT`, `MT`, `ST` followed by `d x w` (digits, optional decimal);
- channels: `C`, `MC` followed by `d x w`;
- angles: `L` followed by `a x b x t` (dimensions may be fractions such as `3/8`);
- hollow sections: `HSS` or `TS` followed by two or three dimensions, optionally with the word `ROUND`/`RND` forms `HSS d x t`;
- pipe: `PIPE` or `P` followed by diameter and `STD`/`XS`/`XXS`;
- plate and bar: `PL` or `PLATE` or `BAR`/`FB` followed by `t x w` (optionally `x length`);
- a bare dimension pair (`d x w`, no family) is its own class, "dimensions only", and is NOT counted as a size in the decision below (it is reported next to it).
Each class is reported separately and as "any steel size".

## 2. Part B2: decision rule, fixed now
A field **carries sizes for most markups** when its whole-value "any steel size" share is at least **50% of the count symbols' tags** (the FreeText tags that belong to the count symbols, linked by the reply-to entry where present, else nearest within 60 pt as in Phase 5t) or of the **count symbols** themselves (Polygon 3 or 8 vertices), in the same field, in at least **10 of the 16 files**. Any other subtype is a **secondary candidate** when it has at least 100 markups and its whole-value share in one field is at least 50% (then tables are built for it and labelled as a different thing, a label highlight and not a count).
- **If a field qualifies:** build quantity tables per (job, page, size) and (job, size) from it, one markup = quantity 1, symbol and tag of the same pair counted once; `Quantity` columns are not trusted unless a slot is numeric on most markups, in which case that slot is reported separately and NOT used as a multiplier in the primary table. Cross-check job-018 against its finished takeoff (rows already extracted to `fixtures/private/target/rows.json`, same ground-truth definition as Phase 8: profile rows, main steel only, canonical sizes): shares of theirs sizes **exact / within 1 / within 20%**, the quantity error rate, sizes missed and extra, and the counts-times-unit-weight error (catalog edition `aisc-v16.0`). Reading, the Phase 8 bars unchanged: usable as a first draft at 70% within 20% and at most 10% weight error; partial at 40% and 25%; otherwise not usable.
- **If no field qualifies:** the markups are stated NOT to carry sizes as a field and cannot serve as size-level ground truth by that route.
- **Derived route (always run, labelled as derived, NOT a field of the markup):** each count symbol gets the size of the nearest size label in the drawing's text layer within **80 pt** of the symbol centre (the same radius and rule the count tool uses for its nearest-label autofill; size labels are text lines that contain a steel size pattern of the classes above, read from the drawing PDF with PyMuPDF and mapped to the takeoff page by the same page map; a symbol with no label in 80 pt is "no size", counted and reported). Quantity 1 per symbol; tags are not counted. This asks: how far can the markups serve if the SIZE comes from our text layer and only the COUNT and PLACE come from the estimators. Cross-check against job-018 exactly as above. It is run for all 16 files for coverage; the agreement figures exist for job-018 only (the only job with a finished quantity takeoff). Whatever the result, the statement is that the markups are a count-and-place ground truth at best, never a size ground truth by themselves.
- **Total-count cross-check (always run, needs no size):** for job-018, the number of count symbols on the mapped pages against the sum of the quantities of the profile rows of the finished takeoff (main steel as in Phase 8, and all profile rows), reported as a ratio and as the per-page counts against the per-page sums of the rows that map to exactly one of our pages (the Phase 8 mapping; a row spanning several pages cannot be split, so this is biased and said to be).
- **Limits:** one job has a quantity takeoff; a symbol may mean a typical member counted once for several, or a piece counted twice on two sheets; takeoff PDFs hold full sets of which ours is a subset (symbols on pages that do not map are reported apart and excluded from the agreement figures); the workbook can hold scope decisions that are not on the drawings.

## 3. Part C: why the 62-piece size is invisible (job-018, diagnostic, no new rule)
**The size:** the size with the largest quantity in the Phase 8 G1 ground truth of job-018 (62 pieces, 26 to 30 per row). It is named only by its pattern family in the docs.
**C1. Rows and sheets.** For the ground-truth rows of that size: the number of rows, their quantities, the number of references per row, the pages they map to (Phase 8 mapping; page indexes only), and how many references are unmapped. For those pages: where does the size text exist? Per page, three classes, checked in this order and reported separately:
1. **text layer**: a `pdf_text` line or word in our database whose normalised text contains the canonical size (family letters, then the two or three dimensions with `x`), after removing spaces and case;
2. **stroke text**: the same test on `glyph` items (stroked lettering read by the glyph reader);
3. **dimensions only**: a text line holding the dimension pair without the family letters (`12x26` for `W12x26`), or a fragment: the family letters with the first dimension in one item and the rest in a neighbour within 20 pt on the same baseline;
4. **nowhere**: none of the above on any of those pages (page-level and project-level).
**Surrounding pattern** around each occurrence (text items whose centre lies within 120 pt of the occurrence on the same page, plus the occurrence's own line): contains `@` spacing (an `@` followed by a distance), `TYP`/`TYP.`, `EQ SP`/`EQ. SP.`/`EQ.`, a count in the form `(n)` or `n-` or `nX` before the size, `O.C.`, `U.N.O.`, a note-style sentence (a line of five or more words), a leader to one line (the occurrence is linked to a line by our linking, or has a structural line within 24 pt). Only the shares of occurrences having each pattern are printed, never the text.
**C2. Parallel member lines in the region.** The anchor of an occurrence is its linked line (from the Phase 8 members); if it is not linked, the nearest line of any class within 80 pt; if none, "no anchor". For an anchor line, the **region** is the rectangle along the anchor's extent (extended by 10% at both ends) and ±**300 pt** across it; sensitivity ±150 and ±600 pt. **Parallel member lines** = lines of the same stroke class as the anchor and at least half its length, within 3 degrees of parallel, whose centre falls in the region, plus the anchor itself. For each of the three half-widths: the distribution (min, median, max) of the parallel line count over the occurrences with an anchor; the product **(number of occurrences of the size, each counted once) x (median parallel lines per occurrence)**, and the ratio of that product to the takeoff quantity; also the ratio of the occurrence count alone. "Approaches the takeoff quantity" means a ratio between 0.8 and 1.25. This is a diagnostic: no rule is built, no threshold is tuned afterwards, and a ratio in the band at one half-width and not the others is reported as such and not as a finding.
**Limits:** one size on one job; the region is a geometric guess, not what the estimator meant; occurrences are read from our text layer, and a size that exists only as a picture is "nowhere".
