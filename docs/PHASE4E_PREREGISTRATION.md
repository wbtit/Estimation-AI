Written and committed **before** any gold link or any rule score exists. It fixes the sample, the split, the candidate generation, the rules, the parameter grid, the tuning criterion and the metrics. Nothing here may be changed after the VALIDATION set has been scored once.

## Labels, sample and split (STEP 4)
- **Label** = one profile occurrence found by the Phase 4d profile parser in a text-layer line of a plan page; lines with more than 12 words (running notes) and the title strip (right 16% or bottom 12% of the page) are excluded and counted.
- **Sample:** up to 40 labels per document, `random.Random(seed).sample` over the labels sorted by (page, y0, x0), `seed = 4000 + document number` (job-002 -> 4002 and so on).
- **Split:** within each document, `random.Random(seed + 500)` shuffles the sampled labels; the first half is DESIGN, the rest VALIDATION. The split is written to disk (`fixtures/private/study/split.json`) before any gold link is made.
- **Gold:** every gold link is **AI-annotated** from overlay images (the label box in red, up to 6 candidate lines numbered and coloured). Allowed values: a candidate number, `none`, `group` (the label applies to a group of members), `missing` (the right member is not among the candidates), `not_member_label` (the string is not a label of a member in the plan; excluded from the denominators and counted). A convention class is recorded per label: `on_line`, `above_or_below`, `rotated_parallel`, `leader`, `other`. A random 10% of the gold links (fixed seed 4999) is written to `human_spot_check.csv` for the owner.
- **Annotation is blind to the rules:** candidates are generated by distance only; annotators do not see any rule output.

**Fingerprints (written before any gold link exists):** `selection.json` sha-256 `63f75e5bf5da61f3bae719a1e3ad7254cb80796442561ce9192a457d8ff1944c`; `split.json` sha-256 `dcb7511ef2be3cb6f6b0a72c162aaa63acb0544bed520c46fa423acfc3014283` (320 sampled labels: 160 DESIGN, 160 VALIDATION, 20 and 20 per document). Pool sizes before sampling: job-002 641, job-004 119, job-005 205, job-008 124, job-009 138, job-010 83, job-016 260, job-020 223 labels; up to 12 labels per document were excluded as running-note lines or title-strip text.

## Candidate generation (fixed)
Structural lines = reconstructed lines (collinear touching segments merged within 1.0 pt and 0.6 pt offset; equal collinear short segments chained into dashed lines) whose stroke class was verified as structural on overlay images (per document and page); minimum length 12 pt. Candidates = the 6 nearest to the label box by box-to-segment distance within 150 pt.

## Rules (fixed) and parameter grid
Every candidate gets `score = distance` plus penalties; the best score wins (ties: smaller distance, then lower candidate number).
- **R1 nearest line:** no penalties (the baseline).
- **R2 parallel alignment:** penalty `p_ang` (pt) when the candidate's angle to the text baseline exceeds `theta_tol`.
- **R3 same-side preference:** with `side_mode = learned`, penalty `p_side` (pt) for a candidate whose side (label above or below the line in the text frame) differs from the document's preferred side. The preferred side is the majority side of the line-gold labels of the DESIGN set of that document; a candidate touching the label or across the baseline has no side.
- **R4 leader following:** a line (any class) with one end within `leader_snap` pt of the label box, followed through end-to-end connected lines up to `leader_max_len` pt in total, whose far end (or an arrowhead near it) lies within `leader_snap` pt of a candidate, overrides the ranking.
- **R5 group rule:** with `group_notes` on, a label whose line contains TYP, TYPICAL, SIM, SIMILAR, U.N.O., EA or EACH is predicted `group`.
Order of application: R5, then R4, then the score ranking (R1 to R3).

**Grid (all combinations, searched on DESIGN only):** `theta_tol` in {5, 10, 20} degrees; `p_ang` in {0, 20, 60} pt; (`side_mode`, `p_side`) in {(off, 0), (learned, 10), (learned, 30)}; `leader` in {off, on}; `leader_snap` in {3, 6} pt; `leader_max_len` in {120, 250} pt; `group_notes` in {off, on}.

**Selection criterion:** the configuration with the highest DESIGN top-1 accuracy over all DESIGN labels pooled across documents (denominator: labels that are not `not_member_label`); ties go to the configuration with fewer features switched on, then smaller penalties. One global configuration is frozen; the per-document preferred side is part of it. Then the VALIDATION set is scored **exactly once**.

## Confidence model (fixed form, thresholds from a fixed set)
Classes: `high` if a leader was followed, or the margin between the best two scores is at least `m_hi` and the best candidate is aligned; `medium` if the margin is at least `m_med`; else `low` (needs review). `m_med` in {5, 10} pt and `m_hi` in {20, 40} pt are chosen on DESIGN as the pair with the highest DESIGN accuracy in the `high` class subject to at least 10 DESIGN labels in `high`; ties: the smaller `m_hi`. Calibration is then reported on VALIDATION.

## Metrics
Top-1 accuracy (prediction equals gold: the same candidate, or `none` for `none`, or `group` for `group`), recall at 3 (line-gold labels whose gold candidate is within the top 3 of the ranking), the rate of `none` and `group` gold cases, the `missing` rate, failure modes with counts, per document and per convention class; also the confidence-class accuracy. Proposed usability bars (to be confirmed by the owner): top-1 at least 95% on validation = usable as green; 80% to 95% = usable with review; below 80% = not usable.

## Out of the registered scope
Columns (STEP 5) and the self-consistency checks (STEP 6) are descriptive measurements with the definitions given in the study document; they are not tuned.
